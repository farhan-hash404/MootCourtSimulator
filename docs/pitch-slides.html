Moot court Court practice — law students argue a mock case in a full courtroom, before a judge, against an opposing counsel, questioning a witness.

The problem

A moot court needs a whole courtroom — and a courtroom needs people.

1
To hold one, you need a proper courtroom setup — a presiding judge, an opposing counsel and a witness.
2
Arranging all of them takes a great deal of time and effort.
3
So a moot court is held only once or twice a year.
4
Most students never get a turn — and never hear how their argument actually held up.

Our solution

CourtSimulator needs one person in the room: the student.

1
A moot court simulator that needs one human — the law student. AI agents play every other role.
2
One AI acts as the judge, one as the opposing counsel, one as the witness.
3
The student can practise whenever they want, 24/7, from wherever they are.
4
When the case ends, they get a proper evaluation and feedback — how well they defended it, and where they need to improve.
